# BezorgFilters — filters for thuisbezorgd.nl

**BezorgFilters adds the price filters that thuisbezorgd.nl is missing: cap the
delivery fee and the minimum order amount, and see at a glance which
restaurants actually fit your budget.**

Thuisbezorgd lets you sort by delivery fee, but it does not let you *cut off*
the expensive options. If you don't want to pay more than €2,50 in delivery
fees, or you don't want to order €25 minimum just to get dinner, you currently
have to scroll past restaurant after restaurant that doesn't fit. BezorgFilters
fixes exactly that.

## What it does

- **Maximum delivery fee** — set a cap (e.g. €2,50) or enter `0` to see only
  restaurants with free delivery. The "free delivery available" badge counts
  as free.
- **Maximum minimum order** — hide (dim) restaurants whose minimum order
  exceeds the amount you want to spend.
- Restaurants that don't match are **dimmed** rather than removed, so the
  page layout stays intact and you never lose the overview. Scrolling,
  cuisines and the site's own filters keep working as normal.
- Your filters **persist** between visits and browser restarts.

## Where you'll find it

The filters appear as a small panel that lives inside the site itself:

- **Desktop:** as an extra section in the filter sidebar, next to the
  site's own filters.
- **Firefox for Android (and mobile):** right below the cuisine row at the
  top of the list — always visible, no digging through menus. It also
  appears inside the filters sheet behind the filter button.
- The toolbar popup offers the same settings from any page and stays in
  sync with the panel on the site.

The panel follows the site's light and dark theme automatically.

## Good to know

- Works on `www.thuisbezorgd.nl` listing pages.
- No account, no setup: install and the filters are simply there.
- **Privacy-friendly:** the add-on collects nothing and transmits nothing.
  Your filter settings are stored locally on your own device and never
  leave it.
- This is an unofficial, community-made add-on. It is not affiliated with
  or endorsed by thuisbezorgd.nl or any other delivery platform.
